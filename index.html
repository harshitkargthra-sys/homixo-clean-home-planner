DISPOSE THOUGHTFULLY

Waste management guide

🥬

Wet waste

Food scraps and other biodegradable kitchen waste. Keep it separate and follow your municipal collection guidance.

♻️

Dry waste

Keep clean paper, cardboard, metal, glass and accepted plastics separate for recycling where local facilities accept them.

⚠️

Special waste

Batteries, e-waste, medicines, sharps and hazardous items need appropriate authorised or municipal disposal routes. Do not mix them with ordinary household waste.
